About Me

Who am I?

I’m a Nanotechnology Engineering student at the University of Waterloo with experience across research, product development, software, and biomedical engineering. Through roles at Siemens Healthineers, a technology startup, and Randox Laboratories, as well as a biosensor capstone project, I’ve developed a strong interest in working across disciplines and turning complex technical challenges into practical solutions.

In my current role on the Systems Improvement team at Randox Laboratories in Northern Ireland, I’ve been developing a full-stack patent portfolio management platform for the company’s patent team. The project has allowed me to combine software development with intellectual property while exploring how technology can improve complex technical and business processes. Living and working abroad has also given me the opportunity to experience a different work culture and broaden my perspective as an engineer.

I’m especially drawn to work at the intersection of engineering, software, healthcare, and intellectual property. My experience at Randox has strengthened my interest in how technical knowledge can extend beyond traditional engineering roles, including areas such as patent law.

Outside of academics and work, I’m a varsity field hockey athlete, former Team Ontario player, and was previously selected to Field Hockey Canada’s U18 NextGen training squad. When I’m not engineering or playing hockey, I enjoy traveling, playing piano, and painting.
